# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last Updated: April 12, 2025**

## 1. INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how YourPlace Inc. ("Company," "we," or "us") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our distributed social media application ("Application"), at https://YourPlace.network, or interact with any of our services.

Your privacy is important to us. This Policy is designed to help you understand what information we collect, why we collect it, and how you can update, manage, and delete your information.

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1 Information You Provide

When you use our Application or services, you may provide us with certain information, including:

- Account information (username, email address, blockchain addresses)
- Profile information (display name, biography, profile picture)
- Content you create, upload, or share through the Application
- Communications with us or other users through the Application
- Information provided when reporting problems or requesting support

### 2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

We automatically collect certain information when you use the Application, including:

- **Usage Data**: Information about how you use our Application, including features accessed, actions taken, content posted or viewed, and interaction patterns.
- **Performance Metrics**: Technical data about Application performance, including load times, response rates, error reports, and crash logs.
- **Device Information**: Information about the device you use to access our Application, including hardware model, operating system, unique device identifiers, and network information.
- **Blockchain Data**: Public blockchain transaction data related to your use of the Application.
- **IPFS Data**: Information stored on the InterPlanetary File System related to your use of the Application.

## 3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- **Provide and Improve the Application**: To operate, maintain, enhance, and provide features of the Application, develop new features and services, and analyze usage patterns.
- **Technical Support**: To diagnose and resolve technical issues, provide customer support, and ensure proper functioning of the Application.
- **Research and Development**: To conduct research and analyze data to improve user experience, develop new features, and enhance Application performance.
- **Security**: To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, abuse, security risks, and violations of our Terms of Service.
- **Communications**: To respond to your inquiries, provide important notices, and send updates about the Application.
- **Legal Compliance**: To comply with applicable legal obligations, regulatory requirements, or legal process.

## 4. DATA SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

### 4.1 Public Information

Due to the distributed nature of the Application, any content you post or share through the Application may be stored on the blockchain or IPFS and is inherently public. This information may be accessible to other users and third-party gateway operators.

### 4.2 Third-Party Service Providers

We may share your information with third-party vendors, service providers, and contractors who need access to your information to provide services to us or on our behalf. These third parties are subject to confidentiality obligations and are restricted from using the information for any purpose other than providing services to us.

### 4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or government agency) or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

### 4.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, bankruptcy, dissolution, reorganization, or similar transaction or proceeding, your information may be transferred or shared as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

## 5. DATA SECURITY

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, destruction, or alteration. However, due to the inherently public nature of blockchain and IPFS, any information stored on these systems should be considered publicly accessible.

Despite our efforts to secure your information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

## 6. YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

### 6.1 Access and Correction

You may access and update your account information through the Application's settings or by contacting us. Due to the immutable nature of blockchain technology, some information may not be deletable once posted.

### 6.2 Data Collection Opt-Out

You may opt out of certain non-essential data collection through the Application settings. However, some basic diagnostic and error data may still be collected to ensure proper functioning of the Application.

### 6.3 Account Deletion

You may delete your account through the Application settings. Due to the nature of blockchain technology, some information associated with your account may remain on the blockchain or IPFS even after account deletion.

### 6.4 Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

- Right to access information we hold about you
- Right to rectification of inaccurate information
- Right to erasure of your information (subject to blockchain limitations)
- Right to restrict processing of your information
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing of your information

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided in Section 10.

## 7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The Application is not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information.

## 8. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than the country in which you reside. These countries may have data protection laws that are different from the laws of your country.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we will take appropriate safeguards to ensure your information receives an adequate level of protection.

## 9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may modify this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you by posting the updated Policy on our website and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the Application after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the new Policy.
